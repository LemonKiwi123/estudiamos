ESTUDIAMOS {{VERSION}}
Flashcards, quizzes and games for learning languages. Use it on your PC and your phone.
=======================================================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  * A Windows 10 or 11 PC with Microsoft Edge (it comes with Windows).
  * Node.js (free). The setup can install it for you.
  * Optional: an iPhone, iPad or Android phone.
  * Optional: Tailscale (free), to use your phone away from home.


INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it, for example in Documents.
     (Right-click the .zip > Extract All.) Don't run it from inside the .zip.
  2. Double-click "Set up Estudiamos.cmd".
       - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" > "Run anyway".
         It says this about any program downloaded from the internet.
  3. Answer the questions. The setup:
       1. checks for Node.js (and offers to install it),
       2. makes a place for your cards,
       3. puts Estudiamos on your Desktop and Start menu,
       4. lets your phone connect on your home Wi-Fi,
       5. starts Estudiamos,
       6. (optional) sets up Tailscale so your phone works anywhere.
  4. Estudiamos opens. Pick a name and picture, and a short tour shows you around.

  From now on, open it with the "Estudiamos" icon on your Desktop.
  You can run the setup again any time. It only fixes what's missing.


USE IT ON YOUR PHONE
  In Estudiamos on the PC: Settings > Phone & sync > "Setup guide".
  It walks you through it step by step:
    * At home: phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi, then scan a QR code.
    * Anywhere: install Tailscale on the PC and the phone, then open your PC's
      secure address on the phone, for example https://my-pc.tail1234.ts.net
  The PC has to be on and awake for the phone to sync.


MOVING ESTUDIAMOS TO ANOTHER FOLDER
  Estudiamos runs a small sync server in the background (Task Manager calls it
  "Node.js JavaScript Runtime"). To move the Estudiamos folder:
    1. Double-click "Stop Estudiamos.cmd" (inside the StudyCards folder).
    2. Move the whole Estudiamos folder (StudyCards and EstudiarData together).
    3. Double-click "Set up Estudiamos.cmd" in the new place. It fixes the shortcuts.
  If you run the setup from Downloads or the Desktop, it offers to copy Estudiamos
  to your Documents folder first.


WHERE YOUR CARDS ARE KEPT
  In the "EstudiarData" folder next to the "StudyCards" folder. The setup makes it.
    * Your cards never go to any company's server. They stay on your PC and your phone.
    * Daily backups are saved in EstudiarData\backups.
    * Moving to a new PC? Copy the whole folder (StudyCards + EstudiarData) to the new
      PC and run "Set up Estudiamos.cmd" there.

  Estudiamos only goes online when you use these features:
    * Word labels (noun, verb...) for unusual words: only that one word is looked up on Wiktionary.
    * Suggested example sentences: looked up on Tatoeba.
    * Email (only if you set it up yourself in Settings).


UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
  Estudiamos checks for new versions once a day and tells you on the home screen.
  Go to Settings > Updates > "Update now". It backs up your cards, installs the new
  version and restarts, in about 30 seconds. Your phone gets it by itself.
    * Every update is checked: it must be signed by the publisher, and every file must
      match, or it's refused.
    * Something wrong after an update? Settings > Updates > "Go back".
    * Downloaded a new .zip yourself? Settings > Updates > "Update from file".
  Your cards (the "EstudiarData" folder) are never replaced by an update.


REMOVING ESTUDIAMOS
  Delete the Estudiamos shortcut from your Desktop and Start menu, then delete this folder.
  (Delete EstudiarData too only if you don't want your cards any more.)
