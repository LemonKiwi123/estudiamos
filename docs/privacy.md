# Estudiamos privacy policy

*Last updated: November 15, 2026*

Estudiamos is a flashcard and language-learning app. It has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**. The developer does not run any server that receives your data.

## What Estudiamos stores, and where

- **Your decks, cards, progress, settings and pictures** are saved on your own computer, in `Documents\Estudiamos\EstudiarData` (Windows) or inside the app on your phone.
- **Phone sync** happens directly between your computer and your phone, over your home network (or your own Tailscale network if you set one up). Your data does not pass through any server belonging to the developer.
- You can protect phone sync with a connection password. Backups are kept in the same data folder on your computer.

## When Estudiamos contacts other services

Only when you use these features, and only what is needed:

| Feature | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Example sentences ("✨ Suggest a sentence", "Add example sentences") | [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) | The word you are looking up |
| Word types ("Label words" / parts of speech), and meanings of words the Sentence Builder doesn't know (🌐 Look up missing words, can be switched off) | [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org) | The word you are looking up |
| Speaking games and spoken spells (microphone) | Your browser's speech recognition (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome) | Your voice, while the microphone button is on, handled by the browser maker under its own privacy policy |
| Reading words aloud | Your browser's or device's voices | The text being read (online voices only) |
| Email (only if you set it up yourself) | Your own email provider | The messages you choose to send, from your own account |
| Feedback, reviews and translation reports | GitHub (opens in your browser) | Only what you type and choose to post |

The microphone is used only while a speaking game or spoken spell is listening. Nothing is recorded or saved. The music 🎙️ Tuner also uses the microphone, but only on your device: the sound is measured right there and is never sent anywhere, recorded or saved. Photos you open in the Art studio's photo helper also stay on your device. Links on the 🔗 Resources page open other websites in your browser; Estudiamos sends them nothing.

The Microsoft Store version gets its updates from the Microsoft Store.

## Children

Estudiamos does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your control

Your data is yours. Delete the `EstudiarData` folder (or uninstall the app on your phone) to remove everything. You can export your decks at any time from inside the app.
